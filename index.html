// Mission Briefing

At ██:██ hours, eight of the Subject's known associates vanished into the night across Cuauhtémoc. Intelligence places them in three squads — two of three, one of two — each holed up in a different bar, refusing to surface until Paul finds them in person.

Your objective: recover all eight before last call. At each stop, check off the squad as you find them — the moment the final name is ticked, the card flips to reveal the clue to your next destination. The crew grows as you go. The operation ends only when all eight are reunited underground.

Handler Jay Landrum carries this file, sets the pace, grants hints, and documents everything for the permanent record (i.e. films it).

Rules of Engagement
  1. Paul reads each clue aloud, then leads. Handler Jay may grant ONE hint per location — at a price of his choosing.
  2. No location may be skipped. No phones used to cheat the riddles (maps for cabs only).
  3. A squad is "recovered" only once every member is found and their challenge is cleared.
  4. Hydrate between stops. One glass of water per bar is mandatory. The Bureau is not liable.
  5. Paul does not pay for a single drink tonight. This is non-negotiable.
