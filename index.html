// The Legend

When the empire fell in 1519, the priests scattered Moctezuma's gold so that no conqueror could ever claim it — each piece hidden across the city and left in the keeping of a guardian. They say that on the Night of the Dead, when marigolds light the path between worlds and the ancestors walk among us, the trail reopens for a single worthy seeker.

Tonight that seeker is Paul. Seven guardians hold the treasure across three sacred sites. Find them all, claim every piece of the lost gold, and earn the greatest prize of all — one unforgettable last night of freedom. At each site, mark a guardian as found; the instant the final one is lit, the codex turns to reveal the verse to the next site.

Keeper Jay Landrum carries this codex, lights the way, grants omens, and records the legend for those yet to come (i.e. films it).

Laws of the Hunt
  1. The Seeker reads each verse aloud, then leads. Keeper Jay may grant ONE omen (hint) per site — at a price of his choosing.
  2. No site may be skipped. No phones used to cheat the codex (maps for cabs only).
  3. A site's gold is claimed only once every guardian is found and the trial is passed.
  4. Honor the dead: one cup of water per site, an offering to the living. The Society is not liable.
  5. The Seeker pays for nothing tonight. The gold provides.

FIRST SITE · VERSE I~8:00 PM

→ TOLEDO ROOFTOP — Av. Chapultepec 461, Juárez

The reading: "toward the sun, city falls away" = the rooftop above Av. Chapultepec; "a Spanish name / keenest blades" = Toledo, the Spanish city famed for forged steel. Opens early, easy to find — a gentle first ascent.

GUARDIANS OF THE SUN: Cyrus Edjlali · Brett Schaffer

Setup: They arrive early, claim a rooftop table, and wear calavera (sugar-skull) masks. Stay scattered until found.

Trial: Each masked guardian hands Paul an unmarked cup (tequila vs. mezcal vs. a wildcard). He must name the spirit. Wrong guess = a forfeit. Both found → light their marigolds → codex turns to VERSE II.

Forfeits: 10 push-ups · serenade a stranger · text his fiancée a cheesy love line · wear the mask till the next site.

SECOND SITE · VERSE II~9:15 PM

→ TLECÁN — Av. Álvaro Obregón 228, Roma Norte (~12 min walk / 5 min cab south)

The reading: "down Obregón" = Álvaro Obregón; "a temple named FIRE in the Mexica tongue" = tle, Nahuatl for fire. Copal incense + Aztec design + agave spirits. No reservations — works for a moving group.

GUARDIANS OF FIRE: Max D · Zach Hollwedel

Setup: They blend into the crowd and only reveal themselves once Paul completes the order.

Trial: Paul must order the flamed/lit cocktail by description only — no name ("the one set on fire"). Tip the bartender to play along. Once served & guardians found, light their marigolds → codex turns to VERSE III.

FINAL SITE · VERSE III — THE TOMB~10:30 PM

→ OWL BAR (hidden speakeasy) — C. Atlixco 94, Condesa

The reading: "the owl, Mictlan's herald / door bears no mark / the wall will reply" = a hidden speakeasy entrance with a password (the owl is the Aztec messenger of the underworld). Closed Sun & Mon — run Thu–Sat. DJ + 4.9 rating. Reserve a group table in advance.

GUARDIANS OF MICTLAN: Blake Bowen · Mahesh Panwar · Henrique Lage

Setup: They wait inside at the reserved table with the "gold." Give Paul only the corner + the password; let him hunt for the door. The final marigolds flip the codex to the victory panel.

PASSWORD: “ LECHUZA ”   (confirm/swap with the bar — Spanish for "owl")

All seven converge with Paul & Jay. The gold is whole. ¡Viva Paul!
