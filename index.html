// Mission Briefing

At ██:██ hours, nine of the Subject's known associates vanished into the night across Cuauhtémoc. Intelligence places them in three groups of three, each holed up in a different bar, refusing to surface until the Subject finds them in person.

Your objective: recover all nine before last call. Each location holds a sealed clue to the next. Recover a squad, clear their challenge, earn the next clue. The crew grows as you go. The operation ends only when all nine are reunited underground.

The Handler carries this file, holds the sealed clues, sets the pace, and documents everything for the permanent record (i.e. films it).

Rules of Engagement
  1. The Subject reads each clue aloud, then leads. The Handler may grant ONE hint per location — at a price of his choosing.
  2. No location may be skipped. No phones used to cheat the riddles (maps for cabs only).
  3. A squad is "recovered" only once all three members are found and their challenge is cleared.
  4. Hydrate between stops. One glass of water per bar is mandatory. The Bureau is not liable.
  5. The Subject does not pay for a single drink tonight. This is non-negotiable.
